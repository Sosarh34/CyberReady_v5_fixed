CYBERREADY? — PROJECT MAP

app.py
  Flask backend, routes, scoring, SQLite access, admin functions.

cyberready.db
  SQLite database containing the question bank and assessment results.

templates/
  The pages shown in Edge.

static/css/style.css
  Visual design: dark + Gen-Z hybrid.

start.bat
  Launches the app from its own folder (working-directory safe).

setup.bat
  Installs requirements from its own folder and reports failures correctly.

IMPORTANT
  This is a local development MVP. Do not expose it to the public internet.
  The admin page is intentionally simple for the capstone lab and will be
  hardened during the security-testing stage.
